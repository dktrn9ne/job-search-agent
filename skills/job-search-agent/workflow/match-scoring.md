# Match Scoring

Score roles from 0-100. Only roles at 85 or above enter the daily shortlist unless the user requests stretch roles.

## Weighted Rubric

| Category | Weight | What To Check |
|---|---:|---|
| Role function fit | 20 | The day-to-day work matches the candidate's proven strengths. |
| Seniority fit | 12 | Scope, title, ownership, and leadership level are appropriate. |
| Domain fit | 12 | Industry, product type, customer type, or business model match. |
| Skills and tools | 14 | Required tools, methods, workflows, and technical fluency align. |
| Evidence strength | 14 | Resume has concrete proof for the job's most important requirements. |
| Impact alignment | 10 | Candidate metrics and accomplishments match the role's outcomes. |
| Location and work model | 8 | Remote/hybrid/time-zone expectations fit. |
| Compensation and logistics | 5 | Pay, travel, start date, and application burden are acceptable. |
| Strategic value | 5 | Company, network, growth path, or market timing is attractive. |

## Score Interpretation

- 99: Rare exact match. Apply immediately with highly tailored materials.
- 95-98: Excellent match. Strong evidence across almost every requirement.
- 90-94: Strong match. Minor gaps or unknowns, still worth prioritizing.
- 85-89: Good match. Apply when strategic, efficient, or needed for daily volume.
- 80-84: Stretch. Hold unless user asks for broader reach.
- Below 80: Reject.

## Penalties

Apply penalties before final ranking:

- -5 to -15 for seniority mismatch.
- -5 to -20 for onsite mismatch.
- -5 to -15 for unclear or low compensation.
- -10 to -25 for required experience the candidate cannot truthfully support.
- -10 to -30 for questionable posting quality.
- -5 to -10 for high-effort application with weak strategic value.

## Required Evidence Mapping

Every shortlisted role needs a short evidence map:

| Job Requirement | Candidate Proof | Resume Location | Gap/Risk |
|---|---|---|---|

If the evidence map is thin for the top three job requirements, the score should usually fall below 90.
