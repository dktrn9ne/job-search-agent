# Evidence-based matching

## Hard gates, before scoring

- Posting: confirm on the employer/ATS page that the requisition is open. Record verification source and timezone-aware time. Unknown or stale verification goes to review; an old posting date alone does not prove closure. Recheck before submission.
- Location: check candidate location against eligible countries/states, office attendance, timezone and travel constraints. “Remote” does not mean worldwide. Apply only current user constraints, not repository defaults.
- Authorization: use validated answers and sponsorship requirements; unknown goes to review.
- Qualifications: map mandatory requirements to evidence. A confirmed non-negotiable mismatch is a reject. Ambiguous requirements, missing evidence, or “degree or equivalent” need assessment, not invented credentials. Preferred requirements affect score rather than eligibility.
- Compensation: distinguish USD annual employee base from OTE, bonuses, equity, total compensation, and contract gross revenue. A posted annual base maximum below the floor fails; a range crossing the floor needs negotiation/verification; a minimum at or above the floor passes pay only. Missing pay, foreign currency, or unclear location-specific bands go to review. Use the applicable location band. Contract/hourly annualizations require explicit hours/week and paid weeks/year and remain illustrations, not guaranteed base. Targets and hard floors are distinct.
- Duplicates: use employer + requisition ID, then canonical employer/ATS URL and aliases. Preserve identifying query parameters. Similar titles are only review signals; multiple distinct requisitions may exist. Inspect merged evidence, favor authoritative current sources, and resolve conflicts. Do not overwrite application history when a listing refreshes.

## Scoring after eligibility

Use 0–100 rubric points, not probabilities. For each category, record awarded points, rationale, and source evidence. Award zero for unsupported alignment; do not treat unknowns as agreement. Avoid a second blanket penalty for a gap already reflected in category points.

| Category | Maximum |
|---|---:|
| Role function | 20 |
| Seniority/scope | 12 |
| Domain | 12 |
| Skills/tools | 14 |
| Evidence for required outcomes | 14 |
| Impact alignment | 10 |
| Location/work model preference | 8 |
| Compensation/logistics preference | 5 |
| Strategic value | 5 |

Interpret 95–100 as exceptional evidenced alignment, 90–94 as strong, 85–89 as good, and below 85 as a separate stretch queue when requested. These are prioritization conventions, not empirically calibrated success predictions. No volume target can alter the score. Unknown hard gates remain review even with 100 rubric points.

Every shortlisted role needs: requirement, mandatory/preferred classification, candidate proof and its source, and gap/risk. Explicitly address the top three requirements. Do not claim stronger experience from keyword overlap alone.

## Useful daily workflow

Refresh profile and application history; search the user's explicit boards plus relevant direct ATS pages; capture new records; deduplicate; verify hard gates; evidence-rank eligible opportunities; prepare the most useful truthful materials; present the review packet; submit only within authorization; log verified results. Continue researching without inventing a match count. Any monitoring cadence or stopping condition comes from the user’s instructions, not this skill.
